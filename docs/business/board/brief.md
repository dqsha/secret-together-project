# Board brief

## The idea
(copied from founder/idea.md)

**Сервис бронирования столов со схемой зала (working name)**

- What it is: a web app (opens by link or QR, no install) where a restaurant guest picks a date, time and number of people, sees the real floor plan and books a specific table in a few taps; the hostess gets a phone app with all bookings grouped by day, statuses (new / expected / seated / left), walk-ins, moving guests to another table, extending time, and a drag-and-drop floor-plan editor (tables can be joined for big groups).
- Who it is for: owners and managers of restaurants, lounges, karaoke bars and banquet venues in Aktobe, Kazakhstan (a city of about half a million people); later other Kazakh cities. Today these venues take bookings by phone and WhatsApp and keep them in a notebook or in their head.
- What it sells, at what price: a monthly subscription per venue, planned at 15 000–25 000 ₸ a month (working price to test: 19 900 ₸/month), plus a one-time setup fee of 30 000–50 000 ₸ for drawing the venue's floor plan and photos. Prices are the founder's first estimate, not tested. (₸ = Kazakh tenge.)
- Where and how: Aktobe first. The founder works with a local restaurant complex (lounge hall, bar, two karaoke VIP rooms) that will be the first user and showcase. Sales are direct: the founder visits venues, shows the working demo on a phone, offers a free first month. Guests reach the booking page from the venue's Instagram bio, 2GIS listing, WhatsApp and a QR code on the tables.
- Budget and constraints: a working prototype exists (guest booking, hostess app, floor-plan editor) but stores data only on one phone; a real server, database and accounts are still needed before it can be sold. The founder is not a programmer and builds with an AI assistant. Budget, available time and whether a developer will be hired are unknown. Kaspi payment is not integrated.

## Other context a stranger would need
- The founder's own first look at the market (from a quick web check, not verified in depth): there are established Russian table-booking SaaS products with floor plans (for example Restoplace, priced at roughly 2 900–5 900 RUB a month per venue in 2026) and Kazakh restaurant POS/accounting systems sold in tenge (for example Quick Resto at roughly 26 000–65 000 ₸ a month, which is a cash register and accounting system, not mainly a booking tool). Kaspi.kz runs "Kaspi Restaurants" for paying the bill by QR code (not bookings). Whether any of these are actively used for bookings by Aktobe venues is not known.
- Features in the prototype: the guest picks a table on the venue's real floor plan; a calendar and an alarm-clock-style time picker; phone field forced to +7 Kazakh format; optional occasion (birthday, date, Kazakh family celebrations); the hostess sees counts of new bookings, a day strip with bookings per date, a timeline bar per booking, one-tap call/WhatsApp; table photos and descriptions; tables that can be joined for big groups, with neighbours blocked automatically.
- The founder's goal: sell it to other venues in Aktobe for a monthly fee.
