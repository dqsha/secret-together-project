# Product lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The product starts from a real moment, which is the guest seeing the actual room and choosing "that table by the window". Phone and WhatsApp bookings can't offer that, and a demo on a phone shows it right away.
- The hostess side covers the evening as it really happens: walk-ins, moving guests, extending time, joining tables for big groups. These details show taste. The Kazakh family celebrations and the forced +7 phone format show it too.
- There is a showcase venue with real complexity (lounge, bar, karaoke VIP rooms), where the experience can be tested every night.
- It needs no install: guests open it from a link or QR code, and the founder controls those entry points (Instagram bio, 2GIS, tables).

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **The hostess goes back to the notebook.** Most bookings will probably still come by phone and WhatsApp. If she has to re-enter each one in the app on a busy Friday, the notebook wins and the floor plan goes stale. Once the plan is out of date, the guest-side promise of booking a specific table is false.
2. **The product doesn't exist yet.** Data lives on one phone. There are no server, no accounts and no sync between the guest page and the hostess. The handoff from guest booking to hostess phone is the central seam, and today it is missing. A lost or double-booked table on the first weekend ends trust with that venue and its neighbours. A non-programmer building with an AI assistant, with no known budget or developer, makes reliability the open question.
3. **It is unfocused.** Guest booking, hostess CRM, floor-plan editor, photos, occasions, joined tables, setup service: that is a lot to make excellent at once. The pitch needs a paragraph. Nobody has said what the one thing is: guests choosing tables, or the hostess replacing the notebook.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Can you say it in one sentence, for the owner and not the guest?
- Walk me through the first five minutes for a hostess on a full Friday. What does she do when the phone rings?
- How does the guest know the booking is confirmed (WhatsApp, SMS, nothing)? What happens if the hostess never sees it?
- What share of bookings at the showcase venue would come through the link and not by phone? Nobody knows this yet. Can you measure it?
- What would you cut for version 1?
- Who fixes it at 9 pm on a Saturday when it breaks?
- Who keeps the floor plan up to date after setup?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. A real server with accounts and live sync between the guest page and the hostess app, plus a named person responsible for keeping it running.
2. The showcase venue runs for at least 4 weeks with the app as its only booking record: no notebook, no lost or double bookings. Phone bookings take one quick step to enter.
3. A one-sentence pitch and a v1 cut down to that sentence.
4. A clear confirmation message to every guest, so the experience doesn't end at "booked" with silence.
