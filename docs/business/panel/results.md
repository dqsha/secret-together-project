These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- I'd need to visit the venue in Aktobe that already uses it, talk to its manager, and see my own hall's scheme working with the owner there. Then I'd try the free month next to the notebook.
- Seeing it running live in another Aktobe venue with the hostess using it every Friday night, and the owner agreeing to a trial where WhatsApp bookings are entered in one tap and I can see staff actually use it for two weeks.
- Увидеть вживую, в заведении в Актобе, которое я знаю, что оно работает у них не первый год, и поговорить с их владельцем; плюс договор с реквизитами и выгрузкой базы.
- Proof from another venue in Aktobe, with numbers, that no-shows dropped noticeably (for example a prepayment or deposit that is taken automatically for table bookings), and a price that is clearly below what one empty table costs me on a Friday.
- Если покажут вживую в заведении, которое я знаю, что это работает с моим WhatsApp и не дублирует iiko, и хостес освоит за один вечер без потерь броней.
- Proof from another venue in Aktobe that no-shows actually fell, for example automatic reminders and prepayment on a table, with the hostess doing nothing extra.
- Proof from another place in Aktobe, with numbers, that online booking brought in extra weekday guests, or booking built into my iiko so my staff don't enter things twice.
- Увидеть вживую в соседнем заведении, что за пятницу брони через ссылку реально приходят и хостес не тратит на это больше времени, чем на тетрадь. Ещё чтобы это сходилось с моей кассой.
- If another owner I know in Aktobe, whom I can call, told me he has used it for months without problems, and I saw it working in his venue.
- Seeing another venue in Aktobe, with a hall like mine, whose no-shows fell and who earned back more than 9 900 tenge in a month. Or a price around 3 000-4 000 tenge.
- Seeing it running in another Aktobe place like mine for a few months, talking to that manager, and getting a written promise that someone answers on Friday and Saturday night, with the hostesses actually using it during the free month.
- Если покажут живьём в заведении в Актобе, что хостес освоит это за 10 минут, и я увижу в первый месяц, что меньше гостей не приходит, или цена будет около 5 000 в месяц.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
