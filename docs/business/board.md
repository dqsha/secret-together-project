# What the board said

**Vote: 3 × FUND IF · average score 4.0 / 10** (Offers 4, Monopoly 4, Product 4)

## Risks more than one member raised (first)
1. **The product cannot be sold yet.** Data lives on one phone; no server, accounts or live sync between the guest page and the hostess. Who keeps it running (a non-programmer, unknown budget) is open. Raised by all three.
2. **The pain may be too small to pay for.** Notebooks and WhatsApp are free; nobody has measured lost bookings, double bookings or no-shows in tenge. (Offers, Product)
3. **Easy to copy.** Floor-plan booking already exists (Restoplace and others); a POS vendor, Kaspi or a bank could add it. No durable advantage yet. (Monopoly, Offers)

## Other risks
- The hostess drifts back to the notebook because most bookings still arrive by phone, and the floor plan goes stale. (Product)
- The product does too many things; there is no one-sentence pitch for the owner. (Product)
- The ceiling in one city may be low. (Monopoly)

## Conditions — checklist for the rest of the pack
- [ ] Real server, accounts and live sync guest ↔ hostess, with a named person responsible for uptime (all three) → ops, cfo
- [ ] Showcase venue runs 4–6 weeks with the app as its only booking record; measure bookings, no-shows, hostess time before/after (Offers, Product) → launch
- [ ] Phone bookings take one quick step to enter (Product) → ops
- [ ] Every guest gets a clear confirmation (WhatsApp/SMS) (Product) → ops, offer
- [ ] Offer rewritten around a measured outcome with an affordable guarantee (Offers) → offer, cfo
- [ ] Done-for-you setup package (floor plan, photos, table QR codes, Instagram/2GIS/WhatsApp links, hostess training) and a test of the setup fee (Offers) → offer, pricing
- [ ] 3–5 (Offers) / at least 5 (Monopoly) other Aktobe venues paying after the free month, with the cost to win each → launch, marketing
- [ ] Local competitor scan: which tools Aktobe venues use, at what price in tenge (Monopoly) → competitors
- [ ] One durable advantage named and a first step shipped (guest network across venues, banquet deposits, Kaspi tie-in) (Monopoly) → offer
- [ ] One-sentence pitch for the owner; v1 cut down to it (Product) → offer, brand

## Where they disagreed
- The Monopoly lens sees Aktobe as a small market that could be owned; the Offers lens worries the market itself is weak (pain not proven). The Product lens doesn't question the market but doubts daily use by the hostess.
- Offers wants a bigger done-for-you package (more in the box); Product wants a smaller v1 (less in the box). Both can be true: a small product plus a done-for-you setup.

## The strongest version the board can see
Not "a booking app" but **"the hostess's notebook replaced, set up for you in a day"**: one reliable book of reservations for venues with VIP/karaoke rooms and banquets, where phone and WhatsApp bookings take one tap to enter, every guest gets a WhatsApp confirmation, and the guest page with a real floor plan is the bonus that brings online bookings. Sold in Aktobe face to face, with the founder's own venue as measured proof. This differs from the pitch: it puts the hostess's daily work first and the guest's floor plan second.
