# Сервис бронирования столов со схемой зала (рабочее название)

- What it is: a web app (opens by link or QR, no install) where a restaurant guest picks a date, time and number of people, sees the real floor plan and books a specific table in a few taps; the hostess gets a phone app with all bookings grouped by day, statuses (new / expected / seated / left), walk-ins, moving guests to another table, extending time, and a drag-and-drop floor-plan editor (tables can be joined for big groups).
- Who it is for: owners and managers of restaurants, lounges, karaoke bars and banquet venues in Aktobe, Kazakhstan (a city of about half a million people); later other Kazakh cities. Today these venues take bookings by phone and WhatsApp and keep them in a notebook or in their head.
- What it sells, at what price: a monthly subscription per venue, planned at 15 000–25 000 ₸ a month (working price to test: 19 900 ₸/month), plus a one-time setup fee of 30 000–50 000 ₸ for drawing the venue's floor plan and photos. These prices are the founder's first estimate, not tested.
- Where and how: Aktobe first. The founder works with a local restaurant complex (lounge hall, bar, two karaoke VIP rooms) that will be the first user and the showcase. Sales are direct: the founder visits venues, shows the working demo on a phone, offers a free first month. Guests reach the booking page from the venue's Instagram bio, 2GIS listing, WhatsApp and a QR code on the tables.
- Budget and constraints: a working prototype already exists (guest booking, hostess app, floor-plan editor) but it stores data only on one phone; a real server, database and accounts are still needed before it can be sold. The founder is not a programmer and builds with an AI assistant. Budget, the founder's available time and whether a developer will be hired are not known yet (open questions). Payment by Kaspi is not integrated.
