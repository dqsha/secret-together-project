## Price range for "booking software for one venue, a month"
At the National Bank rate of 5.31 ₸ per rouble ([informburo, 7 Oct 2026](https://informburo.kz/kurs-valyut/pair/rub-kzt)) — the rate moves, re-check before quoting:
- lowest paid: Restoplace Mini 2 900 ₽ ≈ **15 400 ₸**
- middle: Restoplace PRO 4 900 ₽ ≈ **26 000 ₸**
- highest single-venue tier seen: Restoplace PRO+ 5 900 ₽ ≈ **31 300 ₸**
- free: Restoplace free tier, bank super-apps (venue fees not published), the notebook.
Saby's 8 800–65 100 ₽ is left out of the median because the billing period is not stated.

## Positioning map (text)
Axes that matter to an Aktobe owner: **local and personal (in tenge, in person, Kazakh details) ↔ remote/foreign**, and **hostess tool (runs the evening) ↔ guest channel (brings guests)**.
- Restoplace, Saby: hostess tool + widget, remote/foreign, priced in roubles, support by chat.
- Halyk, Gourmet, Tabley: guest channel (they own the guest), local, tied to a bank or a discount.
- iiko / Quick Resto: hostess-side POS, local integrators, booking is a side feature.
- Notebook: hostess tool, local, free.
- **This business:** hostess tool + the venue's own guest page, local, set up in person.

## What customers complain about
Thin: public reviews of these tools are scarce. One review of Restoplace notes that some users saw technical failures — the app freezing and screens loading endlessly ([vc.ru](https://vc.ru/services/2050213-obzor-servisa-broirovaniya-stolikov-restoplace)) — one source, marked thin. Restoplace's iOS app shows 3.0 from 2 ratings ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/%D0%B1%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B5-%D1%81%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%B2-restoplace/id1500429225?uo=4)) — too few to mean anything. No Aktobe-specific complaints found. To fill this, ask 5–10 Aktobe hostesses in person.

## The gap
Evidence-based, but weak: nobody found **sets the venue up in person in Aktobe, in tenge, with a hostess app built around karaoke/VIP rooms, banquets and joined tables, and leaves the guest relationship with the venue** (unlike bank apps and discount marketplaces, which put the guest in *their* app). Whether owners care enough to pay is untested.

## The threat
- **Restoplace** could be bought by an Aktobe venue tomorrow at about the same price; it already has a free tier.
- **Halyk / Kaspi** can switch on booking for their hundreds of venues with no sales effort; if Kaspi adds booking to Kaspi Рестораны, guest-side booking becomes a commodity.
- **A local iiko integrator** could resell a booking widget with the POS they already install.
